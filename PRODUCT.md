# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Knowledge workers, students, researchers, and builders who need a focused personal workspace for course notes, project roadmaps, literature notes, and personal writing without noisy collaboration or AI popups.

## Product Purpose

LiveLeaf provides a calm, peaceful writing environment and personal knowledge base. It enables users to capture thoughts instantly, organize documents in fluid drag-and-drop tree hierarchies, search keywords across all pages with snippet highlights, and maintain state with continuous auto-save.

## Positioning

A peaceful, single-user block editor focused purely on speed, deep focus, and zero feature-bloat. LiveLeaf rejects AI popups, noisy multi-user collaboration, complex canvas gadgets, and unnecessary configuration in favor of editorial craftsmanship.

## Operating Context

Used daily on desktop and mobile web for capturing quick notes, writing long-form essays, organizing project specs, maintaining reading logs, and reviewing personal journals in distraction-free light or dark mode.

## Capabilities and Constraints

### Confirmed Capabilities
- Tiptap rich-text block editor with slash (`/`) commands and floating Bubble Menu formatting.
- Fluid nested page hierarchy with drag-and-drop tree reordering.
- Fast keyword search across all documents with highlighted snippets (`⌘K`).
- Continuous, real-time auto-saving to PostgreSQL via Prisma.
- Responsive design with seamless light/dark mode theme switching.

### Strict Constraints
- **NO AI features or glowing AI badges.**
- **NO multi-user real-time collaboration or social feeds.**
- **NO whiteboards, infinite canvases, or complex graph nodes.**
- **NO templates, automations, or complex publishing pipelines.**

## Brand Commitments

- **Logo**: Clean green `Leaf` SVG mark beside `LiveLeaf` wordmark without container boxes.
- **Typography**: Confident editorial pairing — serif display headlines (`Newsreader`) paired with clean sans body text (`Inter`).
- **Aesthetic**: Premium, restrained SaaS visual design (Linear / Raycast grade). Dark/Light adaptive color system with high-contrast typography, generous whitespace, and subtle micro-interactions.

## Evidence on Hand

- Working production-grade codebase with Next.js 16, PostgreSQL, Prisma, BetterAuth, Tiptap, Motion, and Tailwind CSS.
- Functioning web application with authenticated workspace (`/d`), sidebar tree navigation, real-time search modal, and live landing page surface (`/`).

## Product Principles

1. **Clarity Over Complexity**: Strip away unnecessary tools, menus, and configurations to preserve cognitive focus.
2. **Speed & Responsiveness**: Every interaction—typing, searching, page switching, or reordering—must feel instant and fluid.
3. **Editorial Craftsmanship**: Elevate typography, spacing, and micro-interactions to create an environment where writing feels rewarding.
4. **Data Ownership & Integrity**: Single-user privacy with continuous background saving so work is never lost.
